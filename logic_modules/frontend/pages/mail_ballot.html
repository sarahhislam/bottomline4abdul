📬 Michigan Permanent Mail Ballot List & Tracking

Michigan's no-reason absentee voting explained. Join the permanent mail ballot list and track your ballot every step of the way.

✅ No-Reason Absentee Voting in Michigan

Good news: Michigan voters approved Proposal 2 in 2022, which established no-reason absentee voting as a constitutional right. Any registered Michigan voter can request an absentee ballot — no excuse or reason required.
How it works: Once you're on the permanent mail ballot list, your clerk will automatically send you an absentee ballot application before every election. You don't need to re-apply each time.

📋 How to Join the Permanent Mail Ballot List

  1. 1
    Check Your Voter Registration
    Visit the Michigan Voter Information Center (mvic.sos.state.mi.us) to confirm you're registered to vote and your address is current.
  2. 2
    Request to Join the Permanent List
    You can request to join the permanent mail ballot list online through the Michigan Voter Information Center, or by contacting your local city or township clerk's office.
  3. 3
    Receive Your Ballot Application
    Before each election, your clerk will mail you an absentee ballot application automatically. Complete and return it to receive your ballot.
  4. 4
    Vote and Return Your Ballot
    Mark your ballot, sign the return envelope, and return it by mail, in person to your clerk's office, or via a secure drop box by 8 PM on Election Day.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a reason to vote absentee in Michigan?
No. Michigan voters approved no-reason absentee voting as a constitutional right. Any registered voter can request an absentee ballot for any reason — or no reason at all.
What is the permanent mail ballot list?
The permanent mail ballot list ensures your clerk automatically sends you an absentee ballot application before every election. You don't need to re-apply each time.
When will I receive my ballot?
Absentee ballots are typically mailed 40 days before Election Day. If you haven't received yours within two weeks of the election, contact your local clerk.
Can I track my ballot?
Yes. Michigan's official ballot tracking portal at mvic.sos.state.mi.us lets you see the status of your absentee ballot in real time.
What if I want to vote in person instead?
You can still vote in person on Election Day even if you requested an absentee ballot. Simply bring your ballot (if you received it) to the polls to be spoiled, or vote a provisional ballot if needed.
