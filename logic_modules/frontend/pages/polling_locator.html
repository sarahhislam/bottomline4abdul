ℹ️ Important Voting Information

Election Day: Polls are open from 7:00 AM to 8:00 PM in Michigan.

Absentee Voting: Any registered Michigan voter may request an absentee ballot — no reason required.

Drop Boxes: Secure ballot drop boxes are available at every clerk's office and many satellite locations.

Voter ID: Bring a photo ID if you have one. If you don't have ID, you can sign an affidavit and still vote a regular ballot.
