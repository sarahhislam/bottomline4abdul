🗓️ Key Michigan Election Deadlines

    5
    Voter Registration Deadline
    Last day to register to vote online or by mail for the November general election.
  • Oct
    30
    Absentee Ballot Request Deadline
    Last day to request an absentee ballot by mail. You can also request one in person at your clerk's office until Election Day.
  • Nov
    3
    Election Day — Polls Open 7 AM – 8 PM
    Vote in person at your polling place, or return your completed absentee ballot to your clerk's office or a secure drop box by 8 PM.
